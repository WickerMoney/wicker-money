any rights in the Wicker Money name, the WickerMoney wordmark, the nest-and-coin

Logo files are provided for the uses below only.

This policy is short on purpose. When in doubt, ask.

## What you can do without asking

- Refer to the project by name, accurately: "built on Wicker Money", "a plugin
  for Wicker Money", "Wicker Money-compatible".
- Name a plugin or package "<your thing> for Wicker Money" as long as it is clear
  it is yours and not an official one.
- Use the name and logo when writing about, reviewing, or linking to the project,
  without altering the logo and without suggesting endorsement.
- Run an unmodified copy of the software or the official container image and call
  it Wicker Money.

## What you cannot do without permission

- Use the Marks in a way that suggests your product, service, fork or plugin is
  official, endorsed by, or affiliated with the project.
- Distribute a **modified** version of the software under the Wicker Money name
  or logo. Forks are welcome; please give them their own name and logo.
- Use the Marks in a company, product, domain or package name, or as your own
  branding.
- Alter, distort or recombine the logo, or use it as an app icon for something
  other than an unmodified build of the project.
- Register the Marks, or confusingly similar names, as trademarks, domains or
  social handles.

## Third-party plugins

The npm scope `@wickermoney/*` and the `wickermoney` organization are reserved
for official packages. Third-party plugins should publish under their own name.
